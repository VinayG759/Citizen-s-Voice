About
KrowdKraft Community is hosting Hacktoberfest Hack Day Bengaluru 2026, a free, in-person Hack Day bringing together developers, students, professionals, open-source contributors and builders for a full day of learning, collaboration and building.

The Hack Day is part of Hacktoberfest 2026 and will focus on open-source AI and hands-on project building.

Whether you’re an experienced developer or exploring open source for the first time, you’re welcome to participate.

Prize tracks
Best Open-Source AI Project

Build an original project that uses open-source or open-weight AI as an important part of how it works. Teams could create an agent skill, build with an open-weight large or small language model, or build or adapt an open-source model harness. These are examples within one challenge, and teams may combine them.

Best Use of Gemma 4

Gemma 4 is a family of open-weight AI models for building intelligent apps. Win a prize for using any of Google’s latest open-weight models via the Gemini API
