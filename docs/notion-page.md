## 1. Welcome and event overview

Hacktoberfest Hack Day Bengaluru 2026 is a free, in-person, open-source AI hackathon for teams of **1 to 3**. Build and demonstrate an original AI solution within the official project development window of **13 to 18 October 2026**. Each team selects one primary track and one problem statement.
