### Track 1: Best Use of Gemma 4

Meaningful use of Gemma 4 must be demonstrated.

- **G1. Autonomous Multi-Agent Collaboration**
    
    Build a solution that incorporates multiple AI agents working together autonomously toward a shared objective. Demonstrate communication, information exchange, delegated responsibilities and coordinated actions.
    
- **G2. Voice-First AI Interaction**
    
    Integrate voice-based intelligence that lets users interact naturally through speech. Demonstrate understanding of spoken requests, conversational context and meaningful responses or actions.
    
- **G3. Multimodal AI Intelligence**
    
    Build a solution that combines information such as text, images or audio to understand context and produce meaningful outcomes. Demonstrate reasoning across multiple input formats.
    
- **G5. Open Innovation with Gemma 4**
    
    Build an original solution in any domain using Gemma 4 as a core intelligence component. Gemma 4 must meaningfully contribute to the solution.
    

### Track 2: Best Open-Source AI Project

Meaningful use of open-source or open-weight AI must be demonstrated.

- **O1. Autonomous Agent Swarms**
    
    Integrate independent AI agents that communicate, coordinate and work autonomously toward a shared goal. Demonstrate meaningful agent-to-agent interactions and collective task execution.
    
- **O2. Adaptive AI with Memory and Self-Correction**
    
    Build an open-source AI solution that retains relevant context, learns from feedback or identifies and corrects mistakes. Demonstrate adaptation over time rather than treating every interaction independently.
    
- **O3. Trustworthy and Verifiable AI**
    
    Integrate mechanisms that improve transparency, reliability or accountability of AI-generated outputs or decisions. Demonstrate how users can understand, verify or evaluate results.
    
- **O5. Open Innovation with Open-Source AI**
    
    Build any original AI-powered solution in any domain, with meaningful use of open-source or open-weight AI technology.
    

### Track participation rules

- Each team chooses exactly **one primary track and one primary problem statement**.
- Any project domain is allowed. You may combine additional capabilities, but clearly demonstrate the primary chosen approach.
- Projects should use the AI technology relevant to their selected track in a meaningful way.

## 8. Rules and fair play

- Teams must remain within **1 to 3 members**. Changes are permitted only when all participating members meet registration and confirmation requirements.
- One confirmed member may represent a team if the others cannot attend.
- Comply with the **MLH Code of Conduct**, open-source licenses and instructions from the organizers and venue.
- Submit original work and accurately describe contributions. Artificial PR activity, misleading submissions or inaccurate participant details may result in disqualification.
- Walk-in participants, on-spot team formation and registrations are not permitted.

## 9. Frequently asked questions

- **01. Can team members be changed?**
    
    Yes. Teams may add, remove or replace members while keeping team size between 1 and 3. All attending members must independently meet the participation criteria and receive confirmation.
    
- **02. What if only one member can attend?**
    
    A team can participate with one confirmed member present, even if other members cannot attend.
    
- **03. Can a team enter both tracks?**
    
    No. Choose one primary track and one problem statement. You may combine AI capabilities within the selected track.
    
- **04. Can we work on an original idea in any domain?**
    
    Yes. The problem statements describe an AI capability to demonstrate, not a prescribed application or industry.
    
- **05. What if ShipWright suggests an incorrect fix?**
    
    Its comments are recommendations. Review them critically and explain why a suggestion is inapplicable, rather than making a risky or unnecessary change.
    
- **06. Where is the submission portal?**
    
    The official submission page will be announced by the organizers. Prepare your public GitHub repository URL and live deployment URL.
